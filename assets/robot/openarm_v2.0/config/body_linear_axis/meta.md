# OpenArm v2.0 linear-axis body

This support replaces the pedestal. The fixed outer column is 564 mm tall. A
single 600 mm prismatic joint moves the nested 300 mm middle and 300 mm upper
sections as one carriage. The upper section is 80 mm by 80 mm. A 120 mm by
120 mm by 20 mm mounting plate, matching the footprint of the fixed bottom
section, sits on top of the upper section. The chest is lowered so the bottom
of its collision mesh rests on that plate.

Mass, inertia, effort, velocity, and damping values are simulation defaults and
must be replaced with manufacturer data before controlling physical hardware.
